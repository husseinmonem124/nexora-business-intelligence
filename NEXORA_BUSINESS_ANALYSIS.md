# NEXORA — Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

Nexora is a fictional German consumer electronics retailer selling computers, tablets, smartphones, monitors, gaming consoles and accessories.

This project analyzes Nexora's 2025 sales data to identify revenue trends, profitable product categories, high-performing locations, customer behavior and opportunities for business improvement.

The project was developed as a Business Intelligence and Data Analytics case study using Python, Pandas, SQL and SQLite.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Nexora's management wants to better understand the company's sales performance.

The main questions are:

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which categories are the most profitable?
- Which products perform best?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- Which cities and regions perform best?
- Which months generate the highest sales?
- What actions should management take to improve performance?

## 11. Business Insights

### Insight 1 — Smartphones Drive Revenue

Smartphones generated the highest revenue of all product categories.

This indicates strong demand for mobile devices and makes smartphone availability an important business priority.

### Insight 2 — Accessories Have Strong Profitability

Accessories generated the highest profit margin at 38.55%.

Although accessories generate less total revenue, they provide strong profitability.

### Insight 3 — Freiburg Is the Strongest City

Freiburg generated the highest revenue among the analyzed cities.

Management should investigate which products and customer groups are responsible for this performance.

### Insight 4 — Baden-Württemberg Leads Regionally

Baden-Württemberg generated the highest regional revenue.

This makes the region an important target for future marketing and inventory planning.

### Insight 5 — May Was the Strongest Month

May generated the highest monthly revenue.

The company should analyze the causes of this increase and prepare for similar seasonal opportunities.

---

## 12. Management Recommendations

### 1. Protect Smartphone Revenue

Maintain sufficient smartphone inventory and prioritize high-performing smartphone products.

### 2. Increase Accessory Sales

Promote accessories alongside smartphones, computers and gaming products.

Bundle accessories with higher-value products to increase average transaction profitability.

### 3. Investigate Freiburg

Analyze customer behavior and product demand in Freiburg to understand why the city performs strongly.

### 4. Focus on Baden-Württemberg

Consider targeted marketing campaigns and stronger inventory availability in the region.

### 5. Prepare for Strong Sales Periods

Analyze May's performance and use the findings to improve future inventory and marketing planning.

---

## 13. Final Business Recommendation

Nexora should focus on three main priorities:

1. Protect smartphone revenue.
2. Increase sales of high-margin accessories.
3. Use regional and monthly sales data to improve marketing and inventory planning.

These actions could help Nexora maintain revenue growth while improving overall profitability.
